## The big picture

Tissue engineering combines **cells**, **scaffolds** and **signals** (the
"tissue engineering triad"), often inside a **bioreactor**, to repair,
replace or model tissue. Most design problems come down to a few quantitative
questions:

1. **How many cells, and how fast do they grow?** (02)
2. **Can nutrients and oxygen reach every cell?** (03)
3. **Is the scaffold porous, permeable and stiff enough, and does it degrade at the right rate?** (04)
4. **What mechanical environment do cells feel in culture?** (05)
5. **Will it integrate, vascularize and survive regulation?** (06)
